What we heard at the retreat
AI is happening.
But in silos.
What we found
Adoption is often individual or at team level — some are ahead, others feel stuck and behind
The most sophisticated workflows are not always visible to everyone
Main barrier: "I don't know where to start" — blank page syndrome
Experimentation feels like extra work, not part of the job
Our response — three pillars
Adoption & Experimentation
Meet people where they are. Help everyone take the next step up.
Organizational Documentation & Governance
Track what gets built. Designate owners and document before sharing company-wide.
Process-First Automation
Evaluate the process before building. Ask "what could be the options?" before committing.

Why this, why now
AI enablement is
70% change management.

Tools are an essential part of the AI journey, but that's the start. Helping people get into the right mindset and feel confident experimenting is the real change management work.

This isn't a new initiative. It's the "Later" phase of the Enabling AI program we launched last year — the part where we scale adoption, spread knowledge, and make sure no one gets left behind.
