Qualitative trends
↑ Getting better over time
Logistics and operations — consistently praised, praise getting louder each year. Real institutional knowledge built.
Agenda format — each year more participant-driven, less frontal. 2026 hands-on sessions among highest-rated elements.
Pacing — less "busy for the sake of being busy," more intentional use of unstructured time.
Internal communication — highest-ever score in 2026 (4.9). Consistent improvement across all three years.
↓ Persistent challenges
Food — low for two years running (3.0 in 2025, 2.9 in 2026). Worth weighting more heavily in venue selection.
Team Time — flagged in both 2025 and 2026 as underdelivering due to lack of preparation and scope clarity.
Strategic follow-through — people leave energized, but retreat themes don't always land in the day-to-day. Concern has intensified in 2026.
! New in 2026
Organizational context — the restructuring created a backdrop of uncertainty that affected how people experienced the content. Some felt it wasn't acknowledged openly enough. The dip in "sense of direction" (3.4) and "theme mindset reinforced" (3.7) scores is likely connected.
Connection and cohesion scores are slightly lower than previous years — consistent with qualitative signals around lower energy and the weight of change.
