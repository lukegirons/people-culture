06Pay Distribution by Quartile
Where men and women sit across the pay spectrum · Global

Each bar shows what % of that gender group falls in each pay quartile — not raw counts. This corrects for having more men than women overall. Quartile boundaries are calculated on the global population in EUR-equivalent terms.

What this tells us
  • 37% of women fall in Q1 (lowest pay) vs. 18.5% of men. This primarily reflects a seniority and representation gap — fewer women at senior levels — rather than underpaying women in equivalent roles.
  • The company holds a Gender Equality Certification, reflecting work on pay equity within comparable roles. The quartile data maps where the pipeline challenge remains.
  • This indicator is explicitly required by EU Directive 2023/970.
Pay quartile distribution · Europe

Bars show % of each gender group in that quartile. Boundaries calculated on global population for consistency.

What this tells us
  • 42% of women in Europe are in Q1 vs. 22% of men — reflecting concentration of women in specialist grades (P2–P4) and male dominance at senior management levels.
Pay quartile distribution · North America

North America has only 6 women — each individual represents ~17% of the female population. Percentages should be read with caution. Quartile boundaries calculated on the global population.

What this tells us
  • Virtually all North America employees fall in Q4 of the global distribution, reflecting US/Canada market salary levels. This is expected and reflects geographic compensation differences, not inequity.
07Methodology & Notes
How to read this dashboard
  • Compensation metric: All figures show annual base salary only (FTE-adjusted for part-time employees). Variable compensation is not included here — bonus structures and on-target earnings are already documented on Confluence by role and level (Variable Compensation Systems). Monthly remote work allowances for fully remote employees are also excluded.
  • Currency conversion: In the Global view, North American salaries (USD) are converted to EUR using the May 2026 reference rate (1 USD = 0.857 EUR / 1 EUR = 1.1673 USD). Europe figures are always in EUR. North America figures are always in USD. Some residual geographic variation in the Global view may reflect salary market differences rather than gender gaps — use the regional views for within-market comparisons.
  • Work level aggregation: Adjacent levels with insufficient data have been grouped: P1+P2, M1+M2, M4+M5, M6+M7+M8. P3, P4, P5 remain separate. This aggregation improves statistical reliability while maintaining meaningful comparisons.
  • Single-gender groups: Some functions and work levels have populations of one gender only. Where the group has at least 3 people, average and median salary are shown for that gender. Top Management pay data is not shown in any view regardless of group size. All other groups below 2 people per gender show headcount only.
  • Mean vs. median: Bar height shows the average (mean) base salary. The dashed horizontal line on each bar shows the median. When they diverge, the median is usually more representative as it is less affected by outliers.
  • Gap direction: When men's average is higher than women's, the gap is positive. When women's is higher, the gap is negative. Both are shown neutrally — some gaps reflect role mix or seniority distribution within a level, not direct pay inequity.
  • EU Directive 2023/970: This dashboard is designed to meet the transparency and information rights obligations of the EU Pay Transparency Directive, including workforce composition, pay gap by category, and quartile distribution disclosures. Under the Directive, every employee has the right to request the average pay levels disaggregated by gender for their own comparable work category. To exercise this right, write to people@growens.io.
  • Data source: Bloom (Workday-based HRIS). Exported June 15, 2026. Next update: December 2026.
Legal context
This dashboard is produced in line with EU Directive 2023/970 on pay transparency. Every employee has the right to request individual pay comparisons by gender category — write to people@growens.io to exercise this right.
Data privacy
All data is aggregated. No individual salary information is disclosed. Groups with fewer than 2 people of either gender show headcount only, with no salary figures. This rule applies consistently across all views. Updated manually from Bloom exports twice yearly.
