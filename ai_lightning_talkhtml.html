AI Lightning Talk #1 · July 1, 2026
Before Claude: Ask Fellow
Building context
starts earlier.
Context doesn't have to start in Claude. Fellow Channels group meetings by topic — and Ask Fellow can query just that Channel. A focused brainstorm before you even open Claude.
Topic channels
Create a Channel per recurring topic — a project, a client, a team ritual
Drop every related meeting into it
Ask Fellow — synthesis across all of them in seconds, scoped to just that context
Shortcuts
Saved prompts — personal or shared with the whole workspace
Brag Doc, meeting prep, public speaking feedback — or build your own
In April I attended a conference and recorded the relevant sessions into one Channel. In 10 minutes, I had a full recap synthesized across all of them — no rewatching, no manual notes.

AI Lightning Talk #1 · July 1, 2026
Take these with you
Copy. Paste.
Try it today.
Meeting prep
"Look at my Fellow notes from the last 3 meetings with [person/team]. What are the open threads? What should I bring up today?"
Avoid recency bias
"Based on my Fellow notes from the last 3 months, what has [person] worked on and contributed? Help me write a balanced performance summary."
Build context for a new project
"I'm starting a new project on [topic]. Here's what I know: [paste doc or notes]. Help me identify the key open questions and what context I should gather before starting."
Weekly synthesis
"Look at everything I've worked on this week from my Fellow notes and Confluence activity. Give me a 5-bullet summary of progress, decisions made, and what's still open."
